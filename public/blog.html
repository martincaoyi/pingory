How these guides are written

Claims here are dated and tied to a named source; where a check stops, the page says so.

  • No simulated crawlers. No page here sends a forged GPTBot user agent and calls it a test; real verification happens by source IP.
  • Dates and sources. Each guide carries a verification date and names the specification behind a claim.
  • Stated limits. Where a check stops, the page says so.

What none of these guides can tell you: whether an AI product will mention, cite or recommend your site. That outcome depends on the model, the question and the moment, and no file on your server records it.
