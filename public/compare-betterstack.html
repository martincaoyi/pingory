Pingory vs Better Stack (2026): An Honest Comparison

Last verified: September 2026. Prices are in USD.

If you are looking for a Better Stack alternative, you already know the product: Better Stack (formerly Better Uptime) is one of the most polished monitoring suites on the market, bundling uptime checks with incident management, on-call scheduling, and log management. This page compares Pingory and Better Stack on the numbers that show up on an invoice — price, monitor limits, check intervals, and status pages — and says plainly where Better Stack still wins.

The short version

  • Better Stack's free tier: 10 monitors at 3-minute checks plus 1 status page. Pingory's free tier: 50 monitors at 5-minute checks. Neither requires a credit card.
  • Pingory's paid plans start at $4/month for 100 monitors with 60-second checks. Better Stack's responder license starts around $29/month (annual billing), and 50 additional uptime monitors cost $21/month on top.
  • At $6/month, Pingory gives you unlimited monitors and 30-second checks. Better Stack's 30-second checks sit on its paid tiers.
  • Better Stack wins on incident management, on-call rotations with phone and SMS alerts, bundled log management, and a beautiful UI.
  • Pingory wins on price per monitor, status page economics, simplicity, 8 UI languages, and open source code (AGPL-3.0) you can self-host.

Where Better Stack still wins

If your outage response involves people being woken up, Better Stack is the stronger product. Its responder license includes unlimited phone and SMS alerts, on-call schedules, escalation policies and acknowledgement flows. It also bundles log management (the former Logtail product), session replay and error tracking — a real observability suite. And the UI is, frankly, excellent. If you are running a SaaS with a team and an on-call rotation, most of Better Stack's price buys exactly that.

Where Pingory wins

If what you actually need is uptime monitoring and a status page, Better Stack's pricing model means you are paying for an incident-management platform you may not use. Its responder license starts around $29/month per seat, and scaling monitors is usage-based on top of that. On Pingory, $4/month gets you 100 monitors at 60-second checks with a public status page included, and $6 removes the monitor cap entirely. Pingory is also open source under AGPL-3.0 — you can inspect the code or self-host it — and ships an interface in 8 languages.

Monitor types

Both cover the essentials: HTTP/HTTPS status, keyword content, ping, TCP port, SSL certificate expiry, DNS records, and heartbeat/cron monitoring. Pingory adds JSON/API assertions on every plan. Better Stack goes further at the high end with Playwright-based browser checks sold as metered minutes — a genuinely different capability class if you need user-journey testing.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free Better Stack alternative?

Yes. Pingory's free plan includes 50 monitors with 5-minute checks and requires no credit card. Better Stack's free tier includes 10 monitors at 3-minute checks plus one status page — better if you mainly want a free status page, worse on monitor count.

Is Pingory cheaper than Better Stack?

Yes. Pingory's paid plans start at $4/month for 100 monitors. On Better Stack, 50 additional uptime monitors cost $21/month — more than Pingory's entire Starter plan — before you even count the responder license that paid tiers are built around.

Does Pingory include a status page?

Yes — a public one from $4/month, with custom domain, white-label and password protection at $6/month. Better Stack includes one status page free; additional public pages cost $15/month and private pages $50/month.

Can I self-host Pingory?

Yes. Pingory is open source under AGPL-3.0 — clone it from GitHub and run it on your own server for free. Better Stack is a managed platform.

When is Better Stack the better choice?

When you need on-call schedules, phone/SMS alerting, escalation policies, or bundled log management. Pingory deliberately does not build pager workflows — if that is your requirement, Better Stack earns its price.
