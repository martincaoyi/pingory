Pingory vs Pingdom (2026): An Honest Comparison

Last verified: September 2026. Prices are in USD.

If you are looking for a Pingdom alternative, you are probably weighing Pingdom's reputation — 15+ years in the business, real user monitoring, detailed page-speed reports — against a bill that starts before you have monitored anything. This page compares Pingory and Pingdom (a SolarWinds company) on the numbers that show up on an invoice — free tier, price per monitor, check intervals, and status pages — and says plainly where Pingdom still wins.

The short version

  • Pingdom has no free plan. Pingory's free tier: 50 monitors at 5-minute checks, no credit card required.
  • Pingory's paid plans start at $4/month for 100 monitors with 60-second checks. Pingdom's Starter plan is around $10–11/month for just 10 monitors.
  • At $6/month, Pingory gives you unlimited monitors and 30-second checks. Pingdom's mid tier is around $25/month for 50 monitors.
  • Pingdom wins on real user monitoring (RUM), page-speed waterfall reports, and 15+ years of brand trust.
  • Pingory wins on free tier, price per monitor, a public status page included from $4/month, and open source code (AGPL-3.0).

Pingdom's price per monitor is the highest of the mainstream tools — roughly $1 per monitor per month on Starter. Pingory's Starter works out to about 4 cents.

Where Pingdom still wins

Pingdom is not trying to be a cheap uptime checker, and it shows. Its real user monitoring tracks how actual visitors experience your site, with geographic breakdowns, and its page-speed waterfall reports remain among the best in the industry. If your job is performance optimization of a high-traffic site — not just knowing when it is down — Pingdom's data is genuinely better, and Pingory does not offer an equivalent. It is also a 15-year-old brand with the enterprise procurement history to match.

Where Pingory wins

If what you need is uptime monitoring with alerts and a status page, Pingdom's entry price is hard to justify: around $10–11/month for 10 monitors, with no free plan to evaluate the product first. Pingory gives you 50 monitors free forever, and $4/month gets you 100 monitors at 60-second checks with a public status page included — a capability Pingdom's core plans do not bundle. At $6 the monitor cap disappears and checks drop to 30 seconds. Pingory is also open source under AGPL-3.0, so you can self-host or inspect exactly what runs.

Monitor types

Both cover HTTP/HTTPS status checks, keyword content checks, TCP port checks, SSL certificate expiry, DNS records, and heartbeat/cron monitoring. Pingory adds ping (ICMP) and JSON/API assertions on every plan. Pingdom goes further at the high end with browser-based transaction checks — multi-step user journeys — and the RUM and page-speed products described above.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free Pingdom alternative?

Yes. Pingory's free plan includes 50 monitors with 5-minute checks and no credit card. Pingdom offers a trial but no permanent free plan.

Is Pingory cheaper than Pingdom?

Yes, by a wide margin. Pingory's paid plans start at $4/month for 100 monitors; Pingdom's Starter is around $10–11/month for 10 monitors. Ten times the monitors, at less than half the price.

Does Pingory include a status page?

Yes — a public one from $4/month, with custom domain, white-label and password protection at $6/month. A comparable public status page is not part of Pingdom's core monitoring plans.

Does Pingory offer real user monitoring?

No — and this is where Pingdom wins. Pingory focuses on uptime, SSL, DNS, keyword, API and heartbeat monitoring. If you need RUM and page-speed waterfalls, Pingdom is the right tool and we will not pretend otherwise.
